Dezan Shira & Associates rebranding to Ascentium

Same trusted expertise. Broader capabilities. One global brand.

Scaling greater heights, together

Dezan Shira & Associates will be rebranding to Ascentium, marking the next chapter in our development as an international business advisory firm.

As part of Ascentium Group, our established teams and capabilities across Asia will become part of one global brand, connecting the regional expertise our clients know and trust with a broader international business services platform.

The transition to the Ascentium brand will take place progressively across our markets. While our name and visual identity will change, our focus remains the same: providing practical, responsive support to businesses operating, investing, and growing across Asia and internationally.

Thank you for your continued trust and partnership as we take this next step together.

What this means for our clients

Continuity of service
Our teams will continue supporting clients throughout the transition, with existing engagements and ongoing matters continuing as usual.
Broader capabilities
As part of Ascentium, clients can benefit from access to a broader range of expertise and capabilities across the group.
A more connected global network
Bringing our teams together under one global brand strengthens our ability to support businesses across markets and throughout their growth journey.

Our global presence

Our international teams will continue to provide the local knowledge, on-the-ground support, and cross-border expertise that have long defined Dezan Shira & Associates, now as part of the broader Ascentium network.

What is changing?

⇒

Over the coming months, you will begin to see the Ascentium name and visual identity across our offices, websites, communications, and other client touchpoints.

As the transition is being implemented progressively across our international operations, you may continue to see both the Dezan Shira & Associates and Ascentium brands during this period.
